    How it works

    The flow

    1. Export from your AI app. You usually get an email with a download link, which often expires after about a day.
      • ChatGPT: Settings, Data controls, Export data. Open data controls
      • Claude: you can't request it in the Claude phone app. Use a browser on your computer or phone: Settings, Privacy, Export data. You get a second email when it is ready: open its link while logged in to the same account, and download conversations-000.zip, the file with your chats. Some Claude exports leave out the text of many messages; the reader marks those, and the one-chat prompt below gets a full copy. Claude's steps
      • Grok: Settings, Data controls, Export account data, then Download account data on your x.ai account page. Open account page For Grok inside X, use the one-chat prompt below.
      • Copilot: Microsoft privacy dashboard, Copilot, Export all activity history (a .csv). Microsoft's steps
      • Gemini: no export this reader opens. Use the one-chat prompt below.
    2. ChatGPT or Claude: open the export here. Tap Open and pick the .zip as you downloaded it (or the conversations.json inside). Tap a chat to read it, Move to folder to sort, and Download .zip for one .md file per chat, in folders. No AI, nothing uploaded, every word kept.
    3. Read here any time. Open a .md file, or the backup .zip to get its list back. Select several .md parts at once to merge them into one chat.

    Chats in projects

    ChatGPT's export leaves out project names. Before you export, start each chat's title with its project, like [Travel] Packing list, and it lands in the folder Travel here. Or sort chats with Move to folder, and turn on Remember my folders so your next export opens sorted.

    Why Markdown?

    An export is data for computers, with every chat in one file. A .md file is plain text: Google Drive or any notes app shows it as it is, one chat per file, in folders you choose.

    Your chat stays with you

    • Files and exports are read inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and this page has no server.
    • Chats are never saved. Only your display choices are remembered on this device, plus chat ID codes and folder names if you turn on Remember my folders.
    • The app's own files stay on your device so it works offline. Your chats never do.

    Install it on your phone

    • Android: in Chrome, menu (⋮), Install app. Then Share a .md file or an export to Chat Reader from your Files app. Not in the list? Reinstall once.
    • iPhone: in Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen, Add. Leave Open as Web App on.
