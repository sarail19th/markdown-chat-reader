How it works

  1. Export or copy your ChatGPT conversation as a Markdown (.md) file.
  2. Tap Open .md file, drop the file on this page, or use Paste text.
  3. Your messages appear on the right and the AI's on the left.

Your chat stays with you

  • The file is read inside your browser. It is never uploaded, and this page has no server that could receive it.
  • Nothing is saved, stored or tracked. Reload or close the tab and the chat is gone. Your style choices are not saved either.
  • When the page opens it downloads fonts and two small reading libraries from public CDNs. Your chat is never part of those requests.

If the speakers do not split

The reader looks for lines such as **You:**, **ChatGPT:**, ### User, ### Assistant, You said: or Prompt: / Response:, with an optional time or model name after them.
